Ten Years of Kona Biking

Our subject human has always had far too many bikes for one person, and the one maker of bikes who furnished them with more bikes than any other was Kona.

Some 'facts' about Kona bikes:

  • "Kona" was simply the name given to the leeward (basically: less windy) side of the islands of Hawai'i.
  • Our subject human has never been anywhere near the islands of Hawai'i, windward, leeward, or otherwise.
  • Our subject human first acquired a Kona bike frame in late 1999. It was an impulse purchase, the frame was too small for them, and the paint fell off if the wind blew the wrong way (it would definitely have done better on the less windy side of an island), but...
